## Syncing

**My PC and phone don't sync on the same Wi-Fi.**
1. Make sure both are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network, and the phone isn't on mobile data).
2. On the PC, if Easy PDF says the network is **Public**: open Windows Settings → Network & internet → your Wi-Fi → set it to **Private**. Windows blocks devices from finding each other on Public networks.
3. Allow Easy PDF through Windows Firewall if Windows asks.
4. Still nothing? Your files still sync through the encrypted relay when you're apart — just a little slower.

**Some files only sync when I'm home.**
Through the relay each file can be up to 25 MB. Bigger files sync directly over the same Wi-Fi — or use **Make smaller** on them.

**How many devices can I connect?**
The free version connects to one other device — your own or a friend's. To connect a different one, remove the one you have first (Devices → Remove).

## Your files

**Where is my library on the PC?**
Microsoft Store version: in **Documents\Easy PDF**. Open, rename and delete files from inside Easy PDF — not by hand in that folder. Uninstalling Easy PDF never deletes it.

**Can I get my files back from a backup?**
Yes — Settings → Storage → **Restore…** and choose the backup (.zip). Restoring only adds files; it never deletes anything. Restoring works in every edition; making backups is part of Easy PDF Pro (from version 3.5).

**I lost my laptop or phone.** *(version 3.5 and later)*
On another of your devices: Devices → the lost device → **Lost or stolen…**. It's disconnected right away, and your other devices disconnect it the next time they sync, so nothing new reaches it.

**Can I search inside my files?** *(Pro, version 3.5 and later)*
Yes — type in the library's search box and Easy PDF also looks inside every PDF, note and comment, and shows the page.

**Crash reports and usage counts** *(version 3.5 and later)*
Easy PDF asks once whether to send them; nothing is sent unless you say yes. Change it any time in Settings → Help & diagnostics. The [privacy policy](privacy.html) lists exactly what they contain.

## Privacy

Easy PDF has no account, no ads and no tracking. When your devices sync or you share a folder, files are encrypted on your device first; the relay can't read them. Read the full [privacy policy](privacy.html).
