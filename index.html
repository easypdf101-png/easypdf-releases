Easy PDF

Read, organize, scan and sync your PDFs between your Windows PC and your Android phone. No account, no ads, no tracking — and everything that travels is encrypted on your device first.

Get it from Microsoft Help & support

Free on the Microsoft Store · Windows 10 (1809) or newer. The Android app is coming to Google Play.

Your PC and phone, in sync

Same Wi-Fi: devices sync directly. Apart: through an encrypted relay that keeps nothing once delivered.

Scan from your phone

Point your phone at a page and the PDF appears on your PC.

Organize

Folders, favourites and search. Merge, split, reorder, rotate and compress PDFs.

Read and mark up

Highlight, draw, add text and sign. Full screen, themes, light and dark.

Share a folder

Share one folder with a friend — they see only what you choose.

Private by design

No account. End-to-end encrypted. In English, French and Spanish.
